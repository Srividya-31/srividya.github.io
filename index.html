Gated Contrastive Multimodal Fusion for negative meme detection

A fusion architecture that aligns text and image embeddings. It fine-tunes DistilBERT and ViT layers, weights each modality dynamically with a softmax, adds attention, and trains on cross-entropy plus contrastive loss to catch subtle negative sentiment. Posts are classed as positive, neutral or negative.

The gap between the two datasets is the finding: plain image-text posts are far easier than sarcastic, humorous memes.
